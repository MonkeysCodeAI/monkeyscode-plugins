# Writing style

- **Reader first.** Who is reading, what do they already know, what should they do next?
- **Lead with the point.** Answer or outcome in the first sentence; details after.
- **Plain words.** "Use" not "utilize"; cut filler ("simply", "just", "very", "in order to").
- **Specific beats impressive.** "Deploys in 40 seconds" (if measured) beats "blazing fast".
- **Active voice, present tense, second person** for instructions.
- **Short sentences and paragraphs;** lists for steps and options; headings that say something.
- **Consistent terms.** One name per concept; match the UI exactly.
- **Microcopy:** buttons are verbs describing the result ("Create workspace"); errors say what happened and how to fix it, without blame; empty states explain value and offer the first action.
- **Honesty:** no invented proof, no dark patterns, no false urgency; disclose costs and limits.
- **Inclusive and global:** avoid idioms and culture-specific jokes; write for translation.
